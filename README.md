1st place Game Jam game made for Atletski klub NoGA and their "Don't play with your health" humanitarian race made in 48 hours.

Subway surfers like game where you dodge enemies or you can flick them off into the stratosphere by sliding into them like in Crash Bandicoot.
Uses physics based movement, and segment based map. It generates the race track itself with premade street segments.
The ribbons are also spawned in a straight line or a slope. There is a minimal and maximal ribbon number to spawn.
Every time you crash into enemies a metaphorical crab gets closer to you, crash into enemies 3 times, and you lose.
Can also be played on mobile phones.


Game Jam Requirements

# "Don’t Play With Your Health!"

The game conveys the message that the greatest victory is the one over our own obstacles such as laziness, idleness, and bad habits. Only activity, determination, and courage can lead us to the ultimate goal: **health**.

This project is directly connected to a real event — a **humanitarian race in Novska (October 19, 2025)** — whose goal is to raise funds for the work of the **Center for Psychological Support of the Association of Women with Cancer "SVE za NJU"**.

---

## Core Concept

The game uses **metaphorical enemies** that represent emotional and lifestyle barriers such as:

- Laziness  
- Idleness  
- Overeating  
- Excessive TV watching  
- Apathy  

These obstacles symbolize everything that negatively affects our health.

---

## Requirements

- The game should be designed for **a broad audience**.
- It must be **short, simple, and intuitive**, so it can easily be played during the event.
- The game must include **visual symbols of the event**, such as:
  - **Atletski klub NOGA**
  - **NOVSKA ZA NJU**
  - **City event Lukovo**
  - **Association of Women with Cancer "SVE za NJU"**
  - **"NE IGRAJ SE SA ZDRAVLJEM"**
- The game should contain **elements that encourage people to become active and overcome their personal obstacles**.
- Obstacles must have **visual and metaphorical meaning** (not generic enemies like black holes or zombies).
- At the **end of the game**, the screen should display the **logo and the race slogan**:

> **"Ne igraj se sa zdravljem!"**  
> *("Don't play with your health!")*

- A **motivational video** must also be created.  
  - The video does not need to be final, as both the game and the video can continue to be improved later.

---

## Potential Benefits / Ideas

- A **retro 2D platformer style** (or another genre, such as a racing game, as long as it follows the theme and idea).
- **Neon, vibrant colors** and a striking visual aesthetic.
- The **main character wears a black shirt with a pink symbol on the chest** (image provided).
- The game should ideally be **available not only on PC but also on mobile devices**.

All genres are allowed, **as long as they follow the theme and core idea**.

---

## Goal

In addition to the game that will be playable during the **Lukovo city event**, the goal is also to create a **short motivational video** that will be presented to the commission.

Just as the humanitarian race encourages **movement, unity, and community**, the game metaphorically shows that **personal obstacles can be overcome**.

The true goal is **not only reaching the finish line — but achieving health**.
